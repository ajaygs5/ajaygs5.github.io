Building Strong Foundations in Software & Web

An honest overview of my academic journey at SRM IST, technical curiosity, and approach to learning software development.

Hello! I am Ajay Kumar, an undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA – Computer Applications) at SRM Institute of Science and Technology in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

My interest in software development started with understanding how programs work from the ground up—writing structured and object-oriented code in C, C++, and Java, exploring backend logic and automation with Python, and learning modern application development with Swift.
